Fresh before/after evidence

The original main and power-rail revision were rebuilt and extracted with the same pinned toolchain and unmodified foundry decks. Device placement and schematic are unchanged. Both core LVS and both extraction-topology LVS checks pass; all 360 fixed-code corner DC points and ten nominal DC/AC cases complete.

M1/M2/M5 source-to-AVDD resistance falls about 95%; PLOAD supply paths fall about 96%. MPFOLD, N3, RB_NL and VBNFOLDLOW have worse source-path resistance. Individual driving-point resistance is not summed operating IR drop.

Startup and remaining qualification

Four new-layout RCC cases complete and pass: 3.3/5 V AVDD, analog-only and simultaneous analog/digital supply cycling, TT/25 C, trim 72, no external load. Cold settling after a 100 µs ramp is about 98–203 µs; restart settling is 48–113 µs. No UIC or artificial state kick is used. Four baseline cases time out after the same 600-second limit and remain incomplete, not electrical failures.

The original 60 dB PSRR criterion remains unmet. Full PVT startup, mismatch/trim yield, loop stability, device-voltage limits, independent PEX, distributed well/substrate RC, EM/IR and slot-4 harness integration remain open. Finite noise spectra imply about 2.2% higher numerical broadband RMS; native integrated totals are NaN on both layouts and remain unqualified. Both versions droop about 103 mV at a 1 µA load. All conditions and limitations are in the detailed review.
