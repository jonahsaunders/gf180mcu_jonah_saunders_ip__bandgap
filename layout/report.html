Floorplan and implementation steps

  1. Audit the circuit and process. Read the final testbench hierarchy, preserve all device multiplicities, collapse only zero-volt measurement probes, and use the requested 1k poly model. Snap three off-grid dimensions consistently in the source schematics; rerun the ten schematic smoke profiles.
  2. Arrange matched devices deliberately. Preserve orientation and equal geometric centroids for seven MOS matching groups. Place Q1 centrally among eight Q2 unit PNPs; keep Q3 nearby. Use foundry PNP GDS. These choices follow the PDK's guidance on identical units, orientation and gradient cancellation. Edge dummies and equalized interconnect remain open. GF180 matching guidance.
  3. Separate switching and precision regions. Put trim logic in a deep-well region with contacted substrate isolation. Keep the resistor ladder beside its switches and leave active devices out of the MIM area. Device taps and the outer ring provide local body/substrate contacts; this does not replace a complete latch-up and substrate-noise review. Latch-up guidance.
  4. Route and reinforce supplies. Route signals on M2–M4 with physical clearance checks. Add 2 µm M5 supply routes, 193 landing sites and 772 via4 cuts across AVDD/DVDD. Add grounded comb shields below the capacitors. Export all twelve core pins. Pin assignment into the eventual harness remains provisional.
  5. Finish manufacturing geometry. Add deterministic COMP and metal fill, keeping precision arrays and resistors clear of metal fill. Respect MIM bottom-plate access and fill keepouts. Run the full decks, including density checks. MIM rules, COMP fill, metal fill.
  6. Verify independently and rebuild. Run schematic-to-GDS LVS, full DRC, geometry-preserving extraction preparation, and a second LVS audit after collapsing the extracted interconnect resistors. Rebuild from the public scripts and compare all GDS layers and labels: zero geometry differences.
  7. Screen analog behavior. Compare schematic, extracted devices, capacitance-only, and RC models; sweep all trim codes, temperature, PSRR, cold start and restart. Screen 45 process combinations at two supplies and four temperatures. Record failed performance targets as failures, even when the simulator completes.

What remains before a shuttle release

  • Integration: obtain a consistent public wrapper/slot allocation, compact or re-floorplan if the smaller envelope applies, and connect power, trim and output pins. Check shared-multiplexer/pad loading and unpowered behavior.
  • Analog refinement: improve sensitive-node routing and PSRR; add and verify appropriate edge dummies; equalize matched parasitics; perform quantitative supply/ground IR and current-density checks.
  • Complete extraction: resolve Magic's false AVSS–DVSS resistor connection. The supplied RC screen explicitly omits both ground resistances, retains signal/supply RC and coupling above 0.1 fF, and uses the project's documented MIM compatibility model. PNP/well-resistor hierarchy restoration has zero fabricated-geometry XOR and independent topology audits.
  • Extractor accuracy: the pinned Magic 8.3.678 predates the sidewall-capacitance correction identified in the KLayout-PEX documentation, which requires 8.3.679 or newer for its Magic wrapper. The capacitance and RC results here are screening evidence and need revalidation with a corrected extractor.
  • Qualification: calibrated trim across PVT, corner startup/restart, stability, noise, mismatch, load response and device-voltage limits, followed by final wrapper-level DRC/LVS. No performance target was relaxed to make this candidate pass.
