1. Fit within the updated draft user area
Approximately 1270 × 410 µm, from an organizer announcement supplied by the designer on 20 October 2026. The existing 614.0 × 307.6 µm core occupies 36.3% of that rectangle. A centered placement would leave 328 µm on each side and 51.2 µm above/below. These are geometric margins, not verified routing or boundary clearances. The centered position is illustrative; no GDS placement, slot, pin assignment or wrapper connection has changed. This draft target supersedes the earlier inferred smaller envelope for planning. Keep the analog core compact and use spare area selectively for matching, isolation, supply routing and harness access.

2. Supply rejection and routing improvement
Simulated PSRR at TT, 25 °C, trim 72, DVDD = 3.3 V, ideal supplies/grounds, and no external load. The upper plots compare schematic, legacy raw RC, and the two layouts using the same conserved-RCC approximation. The lower plots expand the minima; their vertical scales differ. Minima are sampled over 1 Hz–1 MHz. Routing alone adds 0.24 / 0.25 dB. The larger recovery from legacy raw RC comes principally from extraction corrections. The full curves still differ from the schematic. RCC is approximate and omits ground resistance.

Rendering of the actual routed and filled GDS, not a silicon micrograph. GF180MCU-D, five metals, MIM option B, 1 kΩ/□ poly. Core bounds are 614.0 × 307.6 µm. Selected functional labels overlay the geometry. Layout polygons are rasterized at 600 dpi inside the vector exports; dimensions and labels remain vector. The GDS is unchanged by this figure revision. Its existing checks report zero DRC violations and an LVS match. Shuttle integration and full analog qualification remain open.

8. Fixed-code process and temperature screen
All 360 saved DC samples: five MOS × three BJT × three resistor corners, two supplies, and four temperatures. Trim remains 72; there is no per-corner calibration. Columns separate MOS corners; rows separate AVDD. Each temperature contains nine BJT/resistor combinations. Open markers show samples and vertical strokes show their range; coincident samples overlap. Temperatures are plotted exactly, without horizontal jitter. All runs converged; convergence does not establish specification compliance.

9. Simultaneous supply startup and restart
Simulated analog/digital rail cycling and resulting VREF at TT, 25 °C, trim 72. The upper panel shows saved supply waveforms, including DVDD = 3.3 V in both cases; the lower panel shows VREF. High trim inputs track DVDD. There is no forced initial state or startup injection. Conserved-RCC approximation, with ground resistance omitted. Cold/restart settling after the ramp to ±0.5%: 200.5 / 109.3 µs at 3.3 V and 110.6 / 47.4 µs at 5 V.
